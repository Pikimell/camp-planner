# Camp Planner

Demo: [camp-planner-eight.vercel.app](https://camp-planner-eight.vercel.app)

Camp Planner helps organize a camping trip by turning participants, meals, recipes, and ingredients into a coordinated plan.

## What it solves

- Builds a daily meal plan.
- Calculates the total amount of ingredients.
- Creates a shopping list.
- Distributes food between participants.
- Generates a fair duty schedule.
- Shows who must provide each ingredient for every meal.
- Allows organizers to edit generated plans manually.

## Main modules

- Members
- Ingredients
- Meals and recipes
- Meal Plan
- Duty Scheduler
- Food Distribution
- Food Pickup Plan

The goal is to reduce manual planning, avoid confusion around food, and make responsibilities clear to every participant.
